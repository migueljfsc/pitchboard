Privacy Policy

Pitchboard · Last updated 30 August 2026

Pitchboard is a football tactics board that runs in your browser. You can use all of it without an account, and if you do, nothing you draw ever leaves your device. An account exists only so that boards can be saved and found again from somewhere else.

There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone for their own purposes.

Who is responsible

The controller of any personal data described here is Miguel Cardoso, who runs Pitchboard as a personal, non-commercial project. Contact: migueljfscardoso@protonmail.com.

Using Pitchboard without an account

No personal data is collected. The board you are working on is saved in your browser's localStorage so it survives a refresh; it stays on your device and is never transmitted.

Anonymous share links carry the whole board compressed into the part of the URL after the #. Browsers do not send that part to the server, so a board shared this way is never received or stored here — it travels only between you and whoever you send the link to.

Using Pitchboard with an account

Accounts are optional and exist to persist projects and boards. Two ways to create one:

  • Google sign-in. Google confirms your identity and sends back your email address and a stable account identifier. Both are stored. Your Google password is never seen or received.
  • Email and password. Your email address is stored. Your password is not: only an irreversible cryptographic hash of it is kept, which cannot be turned back into the original.

Optionally a display name, if you set one. No other profile information is requested and none is inferred.

What your boards contain

A saved board holds whatever you put in it: project and board names, formations, player shirt numbers, positions, movements, drawings, and any player or team names you type. If you enter the names of real people, that content is personal data about them, and you are responsible for having a proper basis to record it. Board content is stored so it can be given back to you, and is never read, mined, or used for any other purpose.

Publishing a board produces a share link that anyone holding it can open. What you publish is a frozen copy, readable without an account. Nothing is published unless you choose to publish it.

Cookies

One cookie, set only after you sign in. It holds a random session identifier — no personal data — and exists so that you stay signed in. It is HttpOnly and Secure, cannot be read by scripts, expires after 30 days, and is deleted when you sign out. There are no analytics or advertising cookies, so there is no consent banner to click through.

Who else handles the data

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and stores the database, published boards and uploaded images, acting as a processor on instructions. Cloudflare also processes IP addresses and basic request metadata to deliver and protect the service, and keeps short-lived operational logs.
  • Google is involved only if you choose Google sign-in, and only at the moment you sign in. If you never use it, Google receives nothing.

No one else. Data is not sold, rented, or shared for anyone's marketing.

Where data is stored

On Cloudflare's global network, which means it may be processed outside your own country, including outside the European Economic Area. Cloudflare provides safeguards for such transfers, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Account data is kept until you delete the account. Deleting your account deletes everything with it — projects, boards, published boards and their share links, and sessions — immediately and permanently, with no recovery. Deleting a single board does not by itself withdraw a link you previously published from it; delete the published copy as well if that is what you want.

Expired sessions are removed automatically.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, have it deleted, receive it in a portable form, restrict how it is used, or object to it being used. Most of this you can do yourself in the application; for anything else, email the address above. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority — in Portugal, the CNPD.

The legal bases are: performing the service you asked for, when handling your account and boards; and a legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and available, when limiting abuse.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes with an additional secret that is held separately from the database. Session identifiers are stored hashed, so the database does not contain anything that could be replayed to impersonate you.

Pitchboard is a personal project offered without warranty. It is not the right place for confidential information.

Children

Pitchboard is not directed at children under 16, and accounts are not knowingly created for them. If you believe a child has created one, email the address above and it will be removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes affecting account holders will be notified by email.
